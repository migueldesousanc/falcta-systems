Roadmap

From bench demo to field-ready prototype.

Where FMM-L stands today, and what's next.

Project Phase

Prototype in development

FMM-L is a prototype in active development. We're pre-revenue, with no customers yet — our focus right now is bringing up the hardware and validating the four core functions on the bench ahead of our first live demo.

Status Prototype in development
Stage Pre-revenue, no customers yet
Current Focus Bench validation of the encrypted link, relay, MAVLink integration, and GNSS integrity monitoring
Milestones

Three milestones to a field-ready prototype

1

Bench Demo — Nov 2026

FMM-L's four core functions demonstrated on the bench: encrypted C2 link, relay, native MAVLink integration, and GNSS jamming/spoofing detection.

2

Field Test — Dec 2026

First outdoor field test, validating the same functions in real flight and radio conditions.

3

Final Prototype — Mar 2027

Final FMM-L prototype, ready for partner and customer evaluation.

Demonstrator

NÓTUS: where we test it

NÓTUS, Falcata's fixed-wing glider, is the flight demonstrator we use to validate FMM-L in real flight conditions — not a product in its own right. It gives us a controlled, repeatable airframe to put FMM-L's command link, relay behavior, and GNSS integrity monitoring through real-world testing before the module goes on a customer's own vehicle, whether that's a drone or a ground vehicle.
