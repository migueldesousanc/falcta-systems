Product

FMM-L: the mission module that keeps your vehicle connected and on track.

A resilient command link and GNSS integrity monitor for any ArduPilot or PX4 platform.

FMM-L v1

What it does

FMM-L v1 does exactly four things. Nothing more is claimed and nothing more is built in yet.

Capability What It Does
Encrypted C2 Link Command and telemetry travel over an AES-128 encrypted channel between operator and vehicle.
Relay / Mesh FMM-L modules forward each other's traffic, extending the link's range without added ground infrastructure.
Vehicle-Agnostic Works with any ArduPilot or PX4 autopilot over MAVLink — no integration work on the customer's side.
GNSS Integrity Monitoring Detects GPS jamming and spoofing and alerts the operator in real time.
What It Is — And Isn't

Complements your video link. Doesn't replace it.

FMM-L is a resilient command-and-control channel plus a GNSS integrity monitor — not a video datalink. It carries no video and makes no claim to high bandwidth. It complements whatever video or sensor link the vehicle already carries. We don't call it "unjammable"; what we build is a link designed to keep working under jamming, and a monitor that tells the operator when the GPS fix can't be trusted.
