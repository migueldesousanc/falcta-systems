About Falcata Systems

Company

A small, specialized team building tactical-grade hardware without a defense-industry cost structure.

Production Approach

Lean manufacturing

Falcata Systems was founded to close a gap in the defense market: large contractors carry overheads that push unit costs well above €5,000, while commercial off-the-shelf alternatives are barred from tactical use. We close that gap with a lean manufacturing protocol built around three principles:

  • Scalable electronics: 4-layer PCBs with ENIG finish, prototyped in-house via hot-plate thermal processing to protect IP during early development.
  • 3D-printed chassis: Manufactured via local 3D-print farms using aero-grade PLA (LW-PLA) reinforced with carbon fiber.
  • Rapid iteration: This approach removes the need for expensive injection molds, letting us update aerodynamic designs within 24 hours.
What Drives Us

Mission, vision & values

The principles behind every design decision, from the first prototype to the platforms on our roadmap.

Mission

To design and build field-ready UAS and geospatial intelligence systems that keep working where commercial platforms fail — GPS-denied, contested, and mission-critical environments.

Vision

To be the reference lean-manufacturing partner for tactical-grade unmanned systems across Portugal's defense and civil protection forces, and allied NATO nations.

Values

  • Field-tested: Every design decision is validated against real operational conditions, not lab benchmarks.
  • Lean by design: We solve engineering problems with process, not budget.
  • Operator-first: We build for the people who fly and maintain the platform, not the brochure.
