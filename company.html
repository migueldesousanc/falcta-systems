PRODUCTION PROTOCOLS

Falcta Systems was founded to disrupt an archaic defense market. Large defense corporations carry massive overheads and are incapable of producing technology under €5,000[cite: 1]. Meanwhile, cheap commercial alternatives are banned from tactical use[cite: 1].

We achieved our sub-€1,000 price point by adopting a Lean Manufacturing protocol:

  • Scalable Electronics: 4-layer PCBs with ENIG finish, prototyped in-house via hot-plate thermal processing to protect IP[cite: 1].
  • 3D Mesh Chassis: Manufactured via local 3D print farms utilizing Aero PLA (LW-PLA) reinforced with carbon fiber[cite: 1].
  • Rapid Iteration: This method eliminates the need for expensive plastic injection molds, allowing us to update aerodynamic designs in 24 hours[cite: 1].
